For 16–38 mm openings, slots allow the furthest reach and require the greatest safe distance. Round openings are most restrictive up to 50 mm diameter. Above 50 mm all three shapes are treated the same.

Reach around barriers

When a worker's arm reaches over or around a barrier, the ability to flex around it depends on how close the barrier edge is to the "reasonable reach curve" (RRC). Use this calculator to assess whether a barrier provides adequate protection.
